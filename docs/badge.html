badge

A badge that reads live data.

Most badges are a picture someone typed once. This one is an endpoint: it renders whatever the latest benchmark says, so it cannot go stale and cannot be cherry-picked.

What it currently says

Label: verifiability · Message: peers <third-party top> / LGD <our top> · Colour flips to orange if the gap drops below 50 points.

What the badge does and does not mean

  • Does mean: this package's claims are checkable by a third party — license present, commands match the source, index machine-readable, no leaked paths.
  • Does not mean: the skill is useful, correct, popular, or endorsed by anyone.
  • It is not a certification body. It is a public ruler with a public runner.
Measures verifiability, not usefulness or popularity. Low score means evidence is missing, not that the skill is bad. Snapshot at a point in time; the sample is not random.
